All payments are processed securely by Razorpay. We do not store your card, UPI, or bank details. Please refer to Razorpay's Privacy Policy for details on how they handle your payment data.

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us at the email address below. You may also unsubscribe from any marketing emails using the link provided in those emails.

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

This website and its products are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy
