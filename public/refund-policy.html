1. Digital Product — Instant Delivery

Our products are instant-access digital downloads (e-books and related digital files). As soon as payment is confirmed, your download link is generated.

2. All Sales Final

Because the file is delivered instantly upon payment, all sales are final and non-refundable once the download link has been issued, except in the specific circumstances described below or where required by applicable law.

3. When a Refund May Be Considered

We may, at our discretion, issue a refund in the following limited situations:

  • You were charged more than once for the same order due to a technical/payment error
  • The file you received is corrupted, incomplete, or otherwise inaccessible, and we are unable to resolve the issue after being notified

To request a refund under these circumstances, contact us within 7 days of purchase at vivkaushik00@gmail.com with your order details.

4. Cancellations

Because delivery is instant, orders cannot be cancelled once payment has been completed. If you have not yet completed payment, you may simply close the checkout window to cancel your order.

5. How Refunds Are Processed

Approved refunds will be issued to the original payment method via Razorpay. Please allow 5–7 business days for the refund to appear, depending on your bank or payment provider.

6. Contact Us

For any questions about this policy or to raise a refund request, please email us at vivkaushik00@gmail.com.
